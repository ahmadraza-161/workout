## Adding something

1. Confirm the licence permits commercial redistribution. Prefer public domain,
   CC0 or MIT. **Avoid share-alike licences** such as CC BY-SA: they can reach
   into derivative work and are a poor fit for an app.
2. Download once and commit it. Nothing may be fetched at runtime.
3. Add a row here with the source, the licence and where the file lives.
4. Record the decision in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
