| **Licence** | MIT |

Only the path data is used, not the package: the app needs shapes it can
recolour per exercise, not a component. Vendoring keeps it committed and
offline, and avoids a dependency for two data files (ADR-055).

**Two muscles are approximated**, because the source does not draw them
separately. The guide should not be read as more precise than this:

| Our group | Drawn as |
|---|---|
| `lats` | The back view's upper-back shape, which covers the same area |
| `hip_flexors` | The front view's adductors, the nearest thing the source draws |

## What is **not** bundled

- No stock photography, and no images of people.
- No exercise artwork from any other fitness app. The reference screenshots in
  `reference-img/` guide layout only and never ship ([PRD](PRD.md) §9).
- Exercise animations, workout artwork, branding and sounds are generated by
  this project's own scripts in `tools/` and are ours.

## Adding something

1. Confirm the licence permits commercial redistribution. Prefer public domain,
   CC0 or MIT. **Avoid share-alike licences** such as CC BY-SA: they can reach
   into derivative work and are a poor fit for an app.
2. Download once and commit it. Nothing may be fetched at runtime.
3. Add a row here with the source, the licence and where the file lives.
4. Record the decision in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
