## 1. Product Overview

Home Workout is a free Android app that helps adults exercise at home without equipment. It offers guided workouts with animated exercise demos, automatic timers, multi-day challenges and simple progress tracking.

Everything is free and unlocked. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no account. The app works **100% offline**, and all personal data stays on the user's phone.

## 2. Problem Statement

Many people want to exercise at home but give up early because:

- **Most workout apps are not really free.** Key workouts sit behind subscriptions, and free versions are full of ads.
- **Beginners are unsure about form.** Without guidance, exercises feel confusing or unsafe.
- **There is no clear plan.** It is hard to know what to do each day and to stay consistent.
- **Getting started takes too long.** Sign-ups, long forms and data collection put people off before their first workout.

## 3. Goals & Hard Constraints

### 3.1 Goals

- Offer a **complete home-workout app that is 100% free**, with every workout unlocked.
- Make every exercise **easy to follow** with animations, clear instructions and guided timers.
- Help users **build a lasting habit** with weekly goals, streaks, challenges and progress reports.
- Keep the app **simple, fast and private**: no account, no internet, data stays on the device.
- Get a new user from install to their **first workout in under 60 seconds**.

### 3.2 Hard Constraints

| Constraint | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **No network** | No network calls and no INTERNET permission in the release build. |
| **No API keys, no paid services** | Only free and open-source libraries. |
| **No ads, no analytics** | No ad SDKs, analytics SDKs or trackers. |
| **Everything unlocked** | No paywall, PRO badge or "Unlock" button. |
| **2D Lottie only** | Exercise demos are 2D Lottie animations. No 3D. |
| **Assets generated locally** | Every animation, image and sound is generated by the project's own scripts. |
| **Approved packages only** | Ask before adding any package not listed in the spec (§4). |

## 4. Target Users

- Adults **18+** who want to get fit at home
- **Beginner to intermediate** fitness levels
- **Busy people** with 5–30 minutes a day
- People in **apartments or small spaces** who need quiet, no-jumping workouts
- **Privacy-conscious** users who do not want to create an account
- Users with **budget or low-end Android phones**

## 7. Key User Flows

1. **First launch:** Welcome → gender → goal → level → body data (optional) → weekly target and reminder → safety check → **first workout starts** (under 60 seconds).
2. **Daily workout:** Training tab → choose a workout → details → **Start** → get ready → exercise ↔ rest → finish screen (time, calories, streak) → report updates.
3. **Challenge:** Choose a challenge → **Start Day 1** → finish → next day unlocks → progress is saved.
4. **Check progress:** Report tab → history, streak, weight log and BMI.

### 8.2 Workout Rules

Workout contents are **generated from these rules** by a documented build-time script, so the catalog is reproducible rather than hand-typed.

**Structure:** every workout = warm-up block + main block + cool-down block.

| Workout length | Warm-up | Main | Cool-down |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 10 min | 1 | 4–6 | 1 |
| 10–20 min | 2 | 8–12 | 2 |
| Over 20 min | 3 | 12–18 | 3 |

| Level | Exercise difficulty | Work time | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 – Beginner | 1–2 | 30–40 s | 20–30 s |
| 2 – Intermediate | 1–3 | 40–50 s | 15–20 s |
| 3 – Advanced | 2–3 | 45–60 s | 10–15 s |

**Special rules:**

- Main exercises must match the workout's body focus.
- **HIIT** workouts: rest 10–15 s and include cardio exercises.
- **Stretch** workouts: holds only, 30–45 s each, no rest and no cardio.
- **Quiet** workouts: quiet-safe (no-jumping) exercises only.
- Two exercises that work the same main muscle are never placed back to back, except in strength workouts.
- One-sided exercises appear twice in a row (left, then right).
- The total time must match the duration stated in the catalog (±1 minute).

### 8.3 Challenge Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weekly pattern | Train, train, rest, train, train, train, rest |
| Progression | Week 1: level-1 workouts · Week 2: +10% duration · Week 3: level 2 · Week 4: level 2–3 |
| 14-day challenges | The 4-week progression is compressed into 2 weeks. |
| 21- and 30-day challenges | *Assumed:* 21 days = weeks 1–3; 30 days = weeks 1–4 plus 2 days at the week-4 level. Needs confirmation (§15). |
| Single-exercise challenges (Plank, Push-Up, Squat) | Start at a beginner baseline and add about 10% per training day. Rest days keep the previous value. |

## 9. Content & Safety Guidelines

- The app is for adults (**18+**). Onboarding includes a **PAR-Q** health check, and a "not medical advice" disclaimer is always available in Settings.
- **Included:** weight log, BMI, estimated calories burned, and goal-based categories (build muscle, burn fat, keep fit, flexibility).
- **Not included:** calorie-intake tracking, diet advice, fasting timers, before/after photos.
- **No false claims.** No "lose belly fat with 3 moves" (spot reduction) and no "grow taller" promises. Posture workouts are described honestly.
- **No shaming language** such as "no excuses" or "you're falling behind".
- **Original content.** All names, text, images and branding are our own. The reference app is used only for layout and interaction ideas.
- The safe-rate check for extreme weight goals arrives with the weight-goal feature in v2.

## 10. Technical Approach

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Platform | Android on Google Play. Minimum Android 7.0 (API 24). Targets Android 16 (API 36), which Google Play requires for new apps from August 31, 2026. |
| Framework | React Native with Expo, written in TypeScript (strict mode). |
| Connectivity | 100% offline, with no network calls. The release build removes the INTERNET permission; development builds keep it only to connect to the local dev server. |
| App data | On-device SQLite (`expo-sqlite` + `drizzle-orm`). Quick settings and workout snapshots use `expo-sqlite/kv-store`, not MMKV. |
| Exercise animations | Generated 2D Lottie for all 119 exercises. A flat character rig is built in code, each exercise is defined as 2–4 key poses (joint angles), and the poses are interpolated into a smooth loop. One JSON file per exercise, under 40 KB, in two character variants. |
| Other assets | Thumbnails, workout artwork, muscle map, beeps, app icon and splash are all generated locally. They are placeholders designed to be swapped for real art later (same paths, names and sizes). |
| Content | A versioned JSON content pack inside the app, produced by the content generator (§8). |
| Charts and timer rings | `react-native-svg`. Skia is added only if the share-card image export truly needs it, and then kept isolated. |
| Build tools | Build-time generator scripts in TypeScript (Node). No Python (ADR-005; ADR-022 proposed). |
| Dev workflow | Most of the app runs in **Expo Go** for fast iteration. A development build is used for release testing. |
| App identity | Name and package ID live in one constant plus `app.json`, so renaming is a one-line change. Placeholders: `Home Workout`, `com.placeholder.homeworkout`. |

**Main libraries:** `expo-router` (navigation) · `zustand` (state) · `expo-sqlite` + `drizzle-orm` (database) · `lottie-react-native` (exercise animations) · `react-native-reanimated` (UI motion) · `react-native-svg` (muscle map, charts, timer rings) · `@shopify/flash-list` (fast lists) · `@gorhom/bottom-sheet` · `expo-speech` (voice cues) · `expo-audio` (beeps) · `expo-notifications` (local reminders) · `expo-haptics` · `expo-keep-awake` · `expo-file-system`, `expo-sharing`, `expo-document-picker` (backup) · `i18n-js` + `expo-localization` · `lucide-react-native` (icons) · `date-fns` · `jest` + React Native Testing Library.

Architecture, database schema and workout-engine details are in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md). Visual design is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## 14. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Generated animations look basic**, especially floor exercises (push-ups, planks) | Lower perceived quality | One consistent flat style. Poses stored as data so they are easy to tune. Same file and layer names so professional animations can replace them later. |
| **Posing 119 exercises is a lot of work** | Delays in Phase 2 | Reusable base poses (standing, plank, lying on back, lying on front, kneeling) and a preview screen to check each animation. |
| **Offline-only content** | Content fixes need a Play Store update | Accepted for v1. The content pack format is ready for online updates later. |
| **Placeholder name matches the reference app's name** | Store rejection or trademark complaint | Final name confirmed before Phase 11. The name lives in one constant. |
| **Workout interrupted** (call, screen lock, app closed) | Wrong timer or lost progress | Timer based on real clock time, not countdown ticks. Progress saved at every step. |
| **Google Play requirements** | Launch delay | Follow the checklist in §13. Start the 14-day closed test early. |
| **Large scope (about 45 screens)** | Delays | Build phase by phase. P1 features can move to v1.1. |
| **Performance on low-end phones** | Lag or crashes | Small animation files (under 40 KB), one animation playing at a time, fast lists. |
